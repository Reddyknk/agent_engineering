# WidgetWare Business Brief

WidgetWare sells software and services that help manufacturing and industrial-automation companies modernize plant operations and adopt AI-enabled automation.

## Offerings

WidgetWare provides three major products:
1. **Plant Operations Platform**: Connects plant-operational information and helps teams monitor and improve manufacturing processes.
2. **Industrial AI Accelerator**: Helps industrial organizations identify and implement governed AI use cases.
3. **Predictive Maintenance Engine**: Analyzes sensor telemetry from manufacturing equipment to flag maintenance needs before breakdowns occur.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

A company is a plausible fit for WidgetWare's offerings if they meet the following criteria:
- **Employee Count**: At least 5,000 employees.
- **Preferred Industries**: Manufacturing, industrial automation, automotive manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, industrial equipment, or aerospace manufacturing.
- **Regions**: Operates in the United States, Europe, or India.
- **Excluded Industries**: Consumer retail, restaurants, personal services, financial services, and healthcare are strictly out of scope.

## Buying Signals

Key buying signals include:
- Executive hiring (e.g., new AI leadership).
- Active projects or programs (e.g., digital transformation program, plant modernization, manufacturing data initiative).
- Technical hiring (e.g., Generative AI engineers or specialists).

## Human Approval Boundary

To ensure safe operation:
- The SDR system is strictly analytical.
- No automated actions are permitted: sending emails, sending social messages, or editing CRM databases is completely prohibited.
- All outbound outreach or CRM changes require explicit human review and approval.
